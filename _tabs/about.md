I'm **Aminata Mariétou GUEYE**, a Data & AI Solution Architect and Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) with 10+ years of experience designing data and AI platforms on Azure.

This site is where I share **production lessons**: what worked in real projects, what didn't, and why. Every article aims for architecture trade-offs, costs, pitfalls and **code you can run**, with sources on [GitHub](https://github.com/mariposamg).

## What I write about

- **Microsoft Fabric**: Lakehouse and OneLake, Dataflow Gen2, Real-Time Intelligence and KQL, Fabric data agents
- **Microsoft Foundry and Azure OpenAI**: RAG pipelines and RAG evaluation (groundedness, relevance, faithfulness)
- **Agentic AI**: Semantic Kernel and Microsoft Agent Framework, LangGraph, Copilot Studio
- **Platform engineering**: Databricks, Azure Data Lake, Data Factory standards, governance, FinOps and Responsible AI by design

## Field experience

The examples here are drawn from work across industrial manufacturing, logistics, consumer goods, utilities and energy trading: agentic GenAI with RAG, "AI for BI" with Fabric data agents, multi-country Fabric platforms taken from MVP to production, and real-time IoT analytics.
